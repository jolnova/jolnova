HOW PLATFORM COVERAGE WORKS

Your plan decides the reach. You pick the names.

Solo covers one of Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok — chosen by you inside the application. Studio and Agency cover all four at once.

Pick it in the app

On Solo you open Settings and choose Twitch, Kick, YouTube or TikTok. A source from a platform you did not select cannot be added.

Change it when you need to

If a creator you follow moves platform, swap your selection. The change takes effect immediately and the next change becomes available a month later.

Studio and Agency pool the limit

The creator limit is a single total across all four platforms, so you can spend it however you like — fifteen on one platform or a handful on each.

✓

My Files is included with every plan. Processing a video already on your computer is not a platform and never counts against your platform choice — on Solo, Studio, Agency and during the free trial alike.

LIMITS AND DETECTION

The numbers, and how a clip gets picked.

Two things are worth knowing before you choose: how many creators a plan tracks, and what actually decides that a moment becomes a clip.

◉

Creators tracked per plan

  • Solo — 3 creators on the one platform you picked
  • Studio — 15 creators, counted as one total across all four platforms
  • Agency — 50 creators, counted the same way
  • Tracking a creator and recording one are different limits
  • Solo records up to 4 streams at once; Studio and Agency are limited by disk, not by plan
  • Videos you process from My Files are not creators and are not counted
◐

How a moment is found

Jolnova watches three signals in the recording and cuts where they line up:

  • Chat density — how fast messages arrive compared with the calm baseline
  • Audio spikes — laughter, shouting, a sudden jump in loudness
  • Speech content — what is actually being said, from the transcript

Detection sensitivity is a setting you control: Few, Balanced or Many. Every clip still lands in the review queue and you decide what leaves it.

No clip carries an AI score or a quality grade. The AI writes one thing — the title of a clip you approved.

PRICING QUESTIONS

Before you decide

Can I subscribe right now?

Not yet. Payment is not connected, so the checkout buttons are disabled. You can download the application and create an account today.

Do my own video files count as a platform?

No. My Files is included with every plan, including the free trial. Processing a video sitting on your computer never touches your platform selection or your creator limit.

What is the difference between Solo, Studio and Agency?

Solo follows 3 creators on one platform you choose, and gives you 100 approved clips a month. Studio opens all four platforms, raises the limit to 15 creators and 500 clips, and adds the three things a clip page actually needs: uploading to YouTube, sports mode and subtitle translation. Agency is the same product at volume — 50 creators, 2000 clips, 4K export where the source allows it, and priority processing and support.

Subtitles, the subtitle editor, automatic clip detection, the review queue and My Files are on every paid plan.

How many creators can I track?

3 on Solo, 15 on Studio, 50 on Agency. On Studio and Agency that number is a single pooled total across Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok — spend it however you like. Tracking a creator and recording one are different limits: you can follow fifteen channels and have several of them recording at once.

What does "approved clips a month" mean?

It counts the clips you approve, not the ones Jolnova produces. Everything lands in the review queue first and a clip you reject costs you nothing. There is a daily ceiling as well — 8 on Solo, 30 on Studio, 100 on Agency — so a single day cannot burn the whole month.

Can I fix a mistake in the subtitles?

Yes, on every paid plan. Every subtitle line is listed with its timecode and stays editable: correct a misheard word, rewrite the sentence, or delete a line you do not want on screen. Timings stay exactly as transcribed. Saving re-renders the clip once from a clean master, so correcting it twice does not stack quality loss.

What is sports mode?

A second detector for match broadcasts. Instead of looking for laughter and chat spikes it follows the commentary and the crowd, so goals, penalties and red cards come out as clips. It works on full matches and on the highlight packages leagues publish on their own channels. Studio and Agency.

What does subtitle translation do?

It rewrites the burned-in subtitles of an approved clip in another language — English, Turkish, German, French or Spanish — and re-renders the clip once from the clean master. Useful when the audience you want does not speak the language of the stream. Studio and Agency.

What is the platform badge?

The platform name and channel line Jolnova draws on the clip, such as the platform name and the channel handle. Studio and Agency can hide it. Because Jolnova draws it rather than the source, removing it leaves the picture untouched.

What is Clip Context?

It lets you pull the start and end of a clip outwards, taken from the original recording rather than from the cut file. Useful when the setup to a joke sits just outside the detected moment. ±5 sec on Solo, ±10 sec on Studio and Agency.

Is there a limit on links and my own files?

Not on a paid plan — Solo, Studio and Agency all process as many links and local videos as you give them. The free trial is the exception: 2 links in total, not per month, because the trial only lasts three days and a monthly renewal would never arrive.

Can I really export in 4K?

Only on Agency, and only when the source is already 4K — a YouTube video or a recording of your own. Twitch and Kick broadcast at 1080p at most, so a clip cut from a live stream comes out at 1080p whatever your plan says. The plan sets a ceiling; it cannot add resolution that was never in the video.

What decides which moments become clips?

Chat density, audio spikes and the speech content of the recording. Where those signals line up, Jolnova cuts a candidate and puts it in the review queue. You approve or reject every one of them.

If I record 4 streams at once, will my disk fill up?

A live recording uses roughly 5.6 GB per stream per hour, so four at once is about 22 GB an hour. The default cleanup budget is 20 GB, which four simultaneous recordings reach in around 53 minutes.

You can raise or lower that budget in Settings. When it is reached nothing is dropped — further recordings are queued until cleanup frees up space.

Can I turn the watermark off?

Yes, on every paid plan. It is a switch in Settings rather than a paid upgrade. During the free trial it stays on.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 3 days, up to 50 clips in total and 2 YouTube links in total, at 720p and with the Jolnova watermark on. It tracks 2 creators and records one stream at a time. It exists so you can see real output from your own sources before paying anything.

What happens when the trial ends?

Whichever runs out first — the three days or the fifty clips — stops new clip production. Nothing is deleted: everything already in your review queue and clip library stays there and can still be approved, edited and exported. Running out of YouTube links only closes that one route; clips from live sources and from your own files keep coming until the days or the clip cap end the trial.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Plan changes will be available from your account area once billing is connected.

Is AI processing included?

Yes, and there is no separate API key to buy or configure. Worth knowing what runs where: clip detection and transcription happen on your own computer and cost nothing per clip. The only step that leaves your machine is the writing one — the transcript and a short chat summary are sent off so the title of a clip you approved can be written.

Does Jolnova post the clips for me?

Only if you set it up. Connect a YouTube account on the Sharing page, approve a clip and pick a day and time — it goes out then. Nothing is uploaded that you did not approve and schedule, and the file sent is the clip as produced, with no re-encoding.
