CAPABILITIES Windows 10 / 11 · runs locally
Live source tracking
Jolnova checks your Kick, Twitch and TikTok sources continuously. The moment a channel goes live, recording begins by itself — you do not have to be at the computer when it starts. Starter records one stream at a time, Creator two and Pro four.
Vertical formatting
Five layouts fill a 9:16 frame: Auto, Game, IRL, Camera corner and Full screen — set per streamer.
Editable subtitles
Speech is transcribed and burned in. Misheard a word? Fix the line or delete it — on every plan.
Titles written for you
Approve a clip and the AI writes its title from what actually happens in it — not a filename.
HD export
Approved clips download at full quality to any folder you pick, with the watermark on or off.
SUBTITLES

Fix what it mishears.

Open a clip and every subtitle line is there with its timecode. Fix a misheard word, rewrite the sentence, or delete a line you do not want on screen. The clip is re-rendered once from a clean master, so editing twice does not stack quality loss.

  • Edit or delete any subtitle line — on every plan
  • Timings stay exactly as transcribed, only the words change
  • Font, size, colour, outline, placement and a background box on Creator and Pro
  • Live preview on a real frame while you tune the style
Subtitle text

Everything it made lands in one queue.

Every produced clip lands in one queue, grouped by source. Filter by platform, search by title, and approve the ones worth posting.

  • Filter by Kick, Twitch, TikTok, YouTube or your own files
  • Search across titles and sources
  • Approve or reject with a single click
  • Select several clips and act on them together
  • Download approved clips in HD

CLIP EDITOR

What can you change after the cut?

Open any clip and you get the whole picture: the vertical preview, the generated title, description and hashtags, and the tools to change what you do not like.

  • Trim the start and end with two handles
  • Fix the subtitle text line by line
  • Drag an image onto the frame and resize it
  • Add text with position, size, colour and background
  • Remove any effect and get the original clip back
  • No quality loss — effects are re-applied from the untouched source
Clip detail

Your clip, your name on it.

Two things can end up written on a finished clip, and both are switches in Settings rather than paid upgrades.

  • The Jolnova watermark — off on every paid plan
  • The platform badge and channel line — hide it on Creator and Pro
  • The badge is drawn by Jolnova, so removing it leaves the picture untouched
