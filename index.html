SUBTITLES

Transcription is never perfect. So the text stays editable.

Open a clip and every subtitle line is there with its timecode. Fix a misheard word, rewrite the sentence, or delete a line you do not want on screen. The clip is re-rendered once from a clean master, so editing twice does not stack quality loss.

  • Edit or delete any subtitle line — on every plan
  • Timings stay exactly as transcribed, only the words change
  • Font, size, colour, outline, placement and a background box on Creator and Pro
  • Live preview on a real frame while you tune the style
Subtitle text
RUN SCHEDULE

It works when your streamers do — and rests when they do not.

A live recording costs roughly 5.6 GB per stream per hour. Tell Jolnova which days and hours to track, and it stays idle the rest of the week instead of burning disk and bandwidth on an empty channel.

  • Weekly time ranges, several days at once
  • Ranges that pass midnight — 20:00 to 02:00 works
  • One-off exceptions for a single date
  • Your computer's local clock, so daylight saving takes care of itself
