New in this version.

Three additions that change what you can do with a clip.

  1. Viewer clips become reels

    Paste a Kick or Twitch clip link and it is turned into a vertical reel exactly as it was clipped — camera placement, subtitles, audio, export. No moment search: someone already decided that moment was good.

  2. Spor

    Bir maç ya da özet videosu ver. Goller ve diğer önemli anlar klip olarak çıkar; her klip, gelişimin tamamını içine alacak kadar geriden başlar ve skor tabelası telefonda okunacak yere konur.

  3. Scheduled sharing

    Connect a YouTube account, pick a day and a time on the calendar, and approved clips go out on their own. The file uploaded is the clip as produced — no re-encoding, no quality loss.

Bilmesi en az diğerleri kadar yararlı.

Two things people expect from a clipping tool that this one deliberately does not have.
